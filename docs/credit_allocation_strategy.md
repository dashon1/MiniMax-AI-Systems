# Credit Allocation Strategy - AEROS Platform

## Research-Based Credit System Recommendation

Based on comprehensive analysis of AI agent platform pricing models in 2025, here's the optimal credit allocation strategy for AEROS:

## Recommended Credit Allocation

### **Standard Tier (Free)**
- **Monthly Credits**: 100 credits
- **Rationale**: Provides meaningful trial experience while preventing abuse
- **Usage Capacity**: 
  - 20-100 simple tasks (1-5 credits each)
  - 10-20 medium tasks (5-10 credits each)  
  - 2-10 complex tasks (10-50 credits each)

### **Pro Tier (Paid)**
- **Monthly Credits**: 2,000 credits
- **Overage Rate**: $0.05 per additional credit
- **Usage Capacity**: Professional-level usage for serious business applications

## Credit Cost Structure by Task Complexity

### **Simple Tasks (1-5 Credits)**
- Basic agent responses and consultations
- Simple document creation
- Quick research queries
- Basic task automation

### **Medium Tasks (5-15 Credits)**
- Comprehensive research reports
- Multi-step workflows
- Document analysis and processing
- Strategic planning sessions

### **Complex Tasks (15-50 Credits)**
- Full MVP development planning
- Enterprise-level strategic analysis
- Multi-agent collaboration projects
- Comprehensive business plan development

### **Enterprise Tasks (50+ Credits)**
- Complete product development lifecycles
- Large-scale system architecture planning
- Comprehensive market research and validation
- Full-stack application development

## Industry Benchmarking

**Research Findings:**
- **Industry Standard**: 100-1,000 credits for basic tiers
- **Credit Cost**: $0.05-$0.08 per credit for overages
- **Hybrid Model**: Base subscription + usage-based overages performs best
- **Usage Patterns**: 80% of users stay within included limits

## Implementation Benefits

### **For Users**
- **Predictable Costs**: Clear understanding of usage limits
- **Scalable Access**: Natural upgrade path as needs grow
- **Fair Usage**: Pay for what you actually use

### **For Business**
- **Revenue Optimization**: Captures value at all usage levels
- **User Acquisition**: Low barrier to entry with free tier
- **Retention**: Usage tracking provides upgrade triggers
